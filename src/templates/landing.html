Questions

Who can wash the dishes?

Only cleaners compete. Everyone else in the family is an evaluator.

When do points reset?

Every week. The cleaner with the most points becomes the new dictator.

How does scoring work?

After each wash, evaluators look at your photo and give it a score.

What can the dictator do?

The dictator can choose any cleaner to wash the dishes once during the following week. Since there are 7 days in a week and 3 cleaners who wash twice each, the 7th day is free, and the dictator decides who washes on that day. The cleaner must stay in the kitchen until the dictator is satisfied with the work. This wash is recorded on the website as the dictator's wash and judged like any other, so the cleaner has every reason to make sure the dictator is happy.

Ready to wash for the crown?

Cleaners earn points. Evaluators decide who deserves them.
